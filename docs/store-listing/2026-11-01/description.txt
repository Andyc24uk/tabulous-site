Keep your tabs. Clear your desktop.

Too many open Chrome tabs? Too many windows cluttering your taskbar or Mac Dock? Tabulous keeps each project in a saved Workspace so you can close its window and reopen the tabs when you need them.

Switch between work, research, learning and everyday browsing without leaving every project open at once. Find your saved tabs instead of hunting through windows or trying to remember where you left a useful page.

• Save and close Workspace windows, then reopen their saved tabs when needed.
• Organise projects into named, coloured Workspaces and Groups.
• Manage saved tabs and keep different browsing contexts together.
• Recover recent local browser sessions and export portable .tab backups.
• Use the dashboard, toolbar popup or side panel.

Free, local-first organisation
Core Workspace, Group, backup and recovery features are free. No account is required. Your local library remains available offline.

Optional Pro sync
Automatically sync your saved Workspace library between computers and Chrome profiles. Connect each device to your account and choose to enable sync. Offline changes sync when you reconnect, with review when changes need your decision. Live browser windows and local recovery snapshots stay on their own device.
