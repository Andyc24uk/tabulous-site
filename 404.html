Tell us what happened

Include your Tabulous version, Chrome version, what you tried, and what happened instead. For billing, include your account email and a receipt reference if available.

Please do not send passwords, email sign-in codes, payment-card details, or a full Workspace export containing private URLs. Review diagnostics before sharing them.

Protect your Workspaces first

Export a local backup before reinstalling the extension or clearing browser data. Local Workspaces belong to the Chrome profile that holds them.

Accounts and billing
