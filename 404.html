For questions, bug reports or account help, email support@tabulous.tools.

Tell us what happened

Include your Tabulous version, Chrome version, what you tried, and what happened instead. For billing, include your account email and a receipt reference if available.

Please do not send passwords, email sign-in codes, payment-card details, or a full Workspace export containing private URLs. Review diagnostics before sharing them.

Protect your Workspaces first

Export a local backup before reinstalling the extension or clearing browser data. Local Workspaces belong to the Chrome profile that holds them.

Accounts and billing

Sign in to your account to check your verified plan or manage an existing subscription when the billing service is available. A payment redirect is not proof of paid access; payment processing can take time.

Version availability

Tabulous 2.0.1 is prepared for Chrome Web Store review. The existing public listing currently offers version 0.8. See the listing for the version available to install.

Privacy or account deletion

Read our privacy information or request account deletion. We do not publish a support response-time guarantee.
