YOUR CHOICES

Request account deletion.

Email support@tabulous.tools from your account email with the subject “Tabulous account deletion request”.

Before you request deletion

Export a local Workspace backup if you want to keep it. Cancel an active subscription separately through your account billing portal, or ask support for help. Sending a deletion email does not automatically cancel a subscription or immediately remove data.

What happens next

Support will verify that you control the account before arranging removal of the account and synchronised library. Do not send your password, sign-in code or full payment details. We do not offer an unverified instant-delete form.

Local copies and exported files on your devices remain under your control. Some billing records may be retained where legally required, and provider backup removal may take longer. See our privacy information.
