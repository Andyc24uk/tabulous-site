Tabulous terms

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Using Tabulous

Tabulous organises Chrome tabs into saved Workspaces. Keep your own exports of important information. Browser storage, external websites and network services can fail; recovery and cloud backup do not guarantee every tab or historical version can be recovered.

Free and Pro

Local Workspace management remains free. Optional Pro provides the cloud features described on our pricing page. Monthly, Annual, Lifetime and Founder provide identical Pro functionality; Founder also includes a gold profile badge. An account, payment and enabling sync are separate steps.

Prices and payment

Prices are quoted in USD: Monthly $1.99, Annual $14.99, Lifetime $29.99, Founder's Lifetime $19.99. The final checkout states the total and any applicable tax before payment. Public purchasing is not currently open. Only the billing service determines availability and confirmed access. Founder is limited to 100 successful purchases and availability may include temporary checkout reservations.

Subscriptions and Lifetime

Monthly and Annual plans recur until cancelled. Manage an existing subscription through your account when billing is available, or contact support. Lifetime plans are a single purchase for Pro access for the lifetime of the Tabulous service; they do not promise that the service will operate indefinitely. If Pro access ends, local features and local Workspaces remain available.

Refunds and consumer rights

Contact support@tabulous.tools for payment issues, cancellations or refund requests. Statutory consumer rights apply where required; these terms do not remove them. We do not publish a separate unconditional refund guarantee.

Account security and acceptable use

Keep email sign-in codes private. Use the service lawfully and do not attempt to bypass access controls or interfere with other users. We may restrict misuse or service-threatening activity.

Contact and privacy

For questions contact support@tabulous.tools. See Privacy for data handling and Account deletion for removal requests.
